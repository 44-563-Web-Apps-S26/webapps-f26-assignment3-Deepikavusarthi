How to Make a Simple Omelette

  1. Gather ingredients.
    1. 2 eggs
    2. Salt (to taste)
    3. Black pepper (optional)
    4. Oil or butter (1 tsp)
  2. Prepare the eggs.
    1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
    2. Add salt and pepper.
    3. Beat well with a fork.
  3. Cook the omelette.
    1. Heat a pan and add oil or butter.
    2. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
    3. Cook on low heat until set.
  4. Fold the omelette and serve hot.
