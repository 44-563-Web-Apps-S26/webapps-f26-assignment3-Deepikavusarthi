Daring Horse and Vander

My superhero team is called Daring Horse and Vander. The adjective Daring describes bravery and courage. The mammal Horse represents strength, speed, and endurance. The sidekick Vander assists the hero with intelligence and strategy. Together, they protect the city using teamwork and determination.
